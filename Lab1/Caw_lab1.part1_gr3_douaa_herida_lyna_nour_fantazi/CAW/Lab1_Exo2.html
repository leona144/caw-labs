Since October 7, 2024, nearly 9,500 Palestinians remain missing, and according to the Government Media Office, the occupation forces have dropped an estimated 125,000 tons of explosives, resulting in the destruction of more than 88% of the Gaza Strip. Over the course of 700 days since the war began, a devastating 64,232 people have been killed, and 161,583 others have been injured. Additionally, 2,613 families have been completely erased from the civil registry, highlighting the immense human cost and scale of the destruction endured by the civilian population.

The classification of the martyrs according to age group and gender is as follows: 19,424 were children, representing 30.2%, including 400 infants who were born and martyred during the war; 10,138 were women, representing 15.8%, of whom more than 8,150 were mothers; 4,695 were elderly, representing 7.3%; and 29,975 were men and young people, representing 46.7%.
